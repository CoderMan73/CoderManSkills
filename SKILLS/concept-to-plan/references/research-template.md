# Research: <topic>

> **Template**: Fill each section with findings. Cite a link, repository, or documentation for every claim. Replace guidance text with evidence.

## 1. Executive summary

One sentence stating whether the idea is unique, partially unique, or redundant, and why. If a complete existing solution is found, lead with that finding and its confidence level.

## 2. Existing solutions survey

Every relevant project, library, or prior art found. For each: link, brief description, and status (maintained, archived, version gap, license).

## 3. Uniqueness verification

The strongest evidence that the idea is or is not already implemented, tied to specific sources.

## 4. Gap analysis

Where existing solutions fall short of the proposed idea. List the precise uncovered nuances, requirements, platforms, or constraints.

## 5. Foundation candidates (ranked)

Ranked list of projects suitable as a starting point, with fit assessment and caveats (maturity, license, structural fit).

## 6. Unknown areas

What could not be verified and what additional search would resolve it.
